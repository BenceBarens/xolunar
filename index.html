We are XOLunar, a tight-knit audiovisual studio based in Amsterdam. We partner with musical artists and visionary events to tell compelling stories through sound, motion, and design. In an industry saturated with recycled aesthetics and copied trends, we exist to break the cycle. We deliver refreshing, original audiovisuals that push boundaries, without losing sight of the proven formulas that drive engagement.

Ambitions

"XO" stands for Xtra Ocellated, representing our unique, hyper-focused vision and our ability to see things differently. "Lunar" reflects our ambition: we aim for the moon, and we elevate our clients to those same heights.

Our Team & Philosophy

Led by Founder and CEO Dario Verheijen, our core team operates on a strict, no-bullshit philosophy. We make sure every deliverable is quality-assured to ensure our standards are never compromised.

We believe that great work is rooted in great communication. Our approach is built on collaborative creative direction, total transparency, and mutual respect. We don't just work for you; we work with you. Our ultimate metric for success is simple: a final product that both parties are exceptionally proud of.
