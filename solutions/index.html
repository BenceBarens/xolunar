Our Services

We operate across three core disciplines to cover every aspect of your brand's audiovisual footprint:

  • Audio
    Music mixing and mastering, executive production, and vocal engineering.
  • Video
    Music videos, short-form content, announcements and promo videos, after-movies, 2D/3D animation, Spotify canvases, and live stage visuals.

The 360° Package

While we accommodate custom, one-off projects, our true strength lies in end-to-end execution. For artists and events looking for maximum impact with minimal friction, we build full 360° packages.

Example: You provide an unmixed track. We will mix and master the audio, design the cover artwork, create the Spotify canvas, animate the YouTube visualizer and lyric video, shoot the official music video, build your press kit, and cut the Instagram Reels to market it.
